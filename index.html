How a burn actually happens

Community BuyBack

No team wallet decides this. Real usage of Injective apps creates real revenue, and a fixed share of it is converted into INJ that is destroyed on-chain, every month.

STEP 01

Apps earn, protocol collects

Trading and finance apps on Injective generate fees. Under the exchange module's fee design, the protocol's share accrues to a revenue pool that funds the BuyBack — other apps and participants can contribute revenue too.

60%

of exchange fees flow to the protocol side of the split

STEP 02

The community commits INJ

Once a month, participants commit INJ in the Community BuyBack and receive a pro-rata share of the basket of ecosystem revenue. It replaced the old winner-take-all weekly auction in October 2025, so everyone who takes part shares the revenue — not just one winning bidder.

STEP 03

Committed INJ is burned

Every INJ committed in the round is permanently destroyed. It doesn't go to the team, it isn't locked for later, and it can't be re-issued by the burn program. That's how the tally above reached 7.27M.

The honest part most burn posts skip

A burn tally is only half the supply story. INJ is also issued to pay staking rewards, and in the 15 Sep 2026 supply snapshot, total supply stood at 122,895,808 INJ — issuance had still outpaced burns up to that point, with an annualised issuance rate of 4.40% in the range then in effect. So "7.27M burned" is real and permanent, but it does not by itself prove the total supply is already shrinking.

That's exactly why the next change matters: governance proposal IIP-617, the "INJ Supply Squeeze," was approved to permanently double the deflation rate — cutting new issuance while the Community BuyBack keeps burning monthly. Burn Watch will track both sides of that race: burns on this page, and whether they start beating issuance.
